Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

DOER is a habit and challenge tracker. This page says plainly what information the app keeps, where it lives, and what we will never do with it.

The short version: your data exists to run your challenges and nothing else. No ads, no selling of data, no trackers, no session recording. You can erase your account and everything in it, permanently, from inside the app at any time.

What DOER stores

  • Account: your email address, your name, and a securely hashed password (we never see or store the password itself).
  • Your doing: the challenges you create or join, your daily task list and check-offs, streaks, scores and settings.
  • Photos and clips: pictures and celebration clips you choose to attach to your days. They are visible to you and, in group or community challenges, to the members of that challenge.
  • Notifications: if you turn notifications on, the technical subscription needed to deliver them.
  • Community safety: reports you file and people you block.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, in the Asia-Pacific (Mumbai) region, protected by industry-standard encryption in transit. A working copy also lives on your own device so the app opens fast and works offline.

The penguin (AI features)

DOER's penguin companion writes its lines using Anthropic's Claude AI. To do that, a small summary of your day — task names, counts, streaks — is sent to Anthropic's API and used only to generate the penguin's reply. It is not used to train AI models. The same applies when DOER picks an emoji for a task name you typed.

What we never do

  • No selling or renting your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • No advertising and no third-party trackers.
  • No reading of your photos for anything except showing them where you put them.

Sharing inside DOER

Group and community challenges are shared spaces: your name, progress, photos and wall posts in those challenges are visible to their members. Solo challenges are yours alone.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete Account erases everything, permanently: your photos, your history, groups you host, and the account itself. There is no grace period and no backup copy kept. The app also asks you to confirm, because there is genuinely no undo.

Age

DOER is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before the change applies to you.
